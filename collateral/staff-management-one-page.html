How the six work — and the part only a practitioner notices
01

Punch

A photograph and coordinates, taken at the moment. It stores the distance in metres — so forty metres past the boundary and four kilometres away stay different records months later. Strict mode refuses the punch and writes nothing; warning mode allows it and flags the distance. A night shift belongs to the day it began, and punch-out searches two days back to find it.

02

Leave

Applied for in one message, routed to a named administrator, decided within a stated day. Leave filed after it was taken is flagged automatically at the moment of filing — and that same flag is what the appraisal scores months later. The decision carries who approved it, when, and why.

03

Task

Reminders fire a day, six hours, two hours, one hour and thirty minutes before due. Reassign it and the old owner's pending reminders are deleted — nobody is chased about work that stopped being theirs. Recurring duties on six frequencies, and creating the schedule creates the first task the same day.

04

WhatsApp

Ten operations from chat, so the technician who will never install anything still gets counted. Partial names resolve and word order does not matter. Completing a task is refused unless it is yours. Absentees are found by diffing the roster against the punches — a report that lists the attendance table can never show you who is missing.

05

Pay

Payable days ÷ working days becomes the pro-rata factor, applied to earnings only, never to deductions — the line item that quietly under-deducts provident fund. PF on Basic+DA capped at ₹15,000; ESIC within ₹21,000; professional tax on real state slabs. If the CTC already lists a statutory line, the computed one is zeroed. No double deduction.

06

Appraisal

Thirty-one measured facts per person across tasks, attendance, leave and conversations. Completion 30%, attendance and punctuality 25% each, leave planning and communication 10%. The weighting normalises over the data that exists, so nobody is marked down for a category they were never measured in. Prints as a PDF with the top three named.

What you are losing today
  • The 2nd of the month. Two registers, one Excel sheet, and a supervisor who will "send the night-shift numbers by evening." Payroll waits for him.
  • The man who punched in at 9:50 pm and out at 6:10 am reads as two half days. You fix it by hand. Every month. For every one of them.
  • Salary day, and a message: sir, ₹1,840 kam aaya. Nobody can say which day was cut. You pay the difference to end it.
  • The leave "informed on WhatsApp" in April, now four thousand messages up a group chat. No record, no argument, so the day is paid.
What it refuses to allow
  • Payroll on an open month. period must be locked to finalize
  • Paying anyone with no salary structure on record for that month.
  • Completing a colleague's task from chat. only tasks assigned to you
  • Approving leave without being an admin — and you only ever see requests routed to you.
  • Assigning work across organisations. assignee does not belong
  • Acting at all from a deactivated account. user is inactive
  • A second punch-in while one is open; a punch-out with nothing open.
  • Deleting a task without admin rights — held in the database, not by hiding a button.
