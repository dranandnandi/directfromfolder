Mechanism

How each of the six works — and the part only a practitioner notices.

Everything below is in the running product. Each entry carries one detail you would only think to ask about if you had already done this job by hand.

01

Punch

Attendance · shifts · geofence

The punch carries its own proof

The front camera takes a photograph, the device records its coordinates, and both are written onto the attendance record with a readable street address and the device that sent them.

The detail: it stores the distance from your premises in metres — not merely inside or outside. A punch forty metres past the boundary and one four kilometres away stay distinguishable six months later, when the argument actually happens. You set the posture per organisation: strict mode refuses the punch and writes nothing; warning mode allows it and flags the distance for a supervisor. The boundary adjusts from a hundred metres to two kilometres.

A night shift stays one shift

An attendance beginning at ten at night and ending at six in the morning is filed against the day it began, not split across two dates.

The detail: punch-out looks back two days to find the open punch instead of assuming today, and refuses to close a night shift under six hours or over eighteen — the two shapes a forgotten punch actually takes.

Shift changes are dated, not swapped

Move someone to a new shift from a chosen date. Assignments carry an effective-from and an effective-to, so last month's punctuality is judged against last month's shift.

The detail: the previous assignment closes automatically the day before the new one starts. Nobody is ever on two shifts on one date — the condition that silently corrupts every late report.

Somebody forgot to punch out

They always do. A supervisor closes every session left open beyond a chosen number of hours, in one action.

The detail: each closure is written as a regularisation carrying who did it, why and when — the correction is a record in its own right, not an edit that erases what was there. Regularised days are then excluded from the late and early-out counts that feed the appraisal.
02

Leave

Request · route · decide · record

Applied for in one message, routed to a real person

Full day, half day or early departure, with dates and a reason. The request is routed to an administrator of that organisation and the employee is told who will review it and that a decision is due within twenty-four hours.

An empty reason is refused and malformed dates are refused. If the organisation has no administrator to route to, the request is rejected rather than filed into nothing.

Back-dated leave is flagged the moment it is filed

A request whose start date has already passed is marked post-facto automatically — titled as such, marked in the body of the record, and triaged at a different priority from a planned request. An emergency is raised to critical.

The detail: that same post-facto flag is what the appraisal scores months later. Leave planning is measured by how much leave was filed after it was taken — not by how much leave was taken. Which is the actual problem, and the one no attendance report has ever shown you.

The decision is signed

Approve or reject, with a note. The record keeps a decision block — decided by, decided at, and the note — and the employee is notified with the approver named.

The detail: an administrator can only act on requests routed to them, which is why approving your own leave is not a rule that can be bent — the request was never in your queue to begin with.
03

Task

Assign · remind · recur · prove

The reminder ladder

Give a task a due date and reminders are scheduled the instant it is saved — one day before, then six hours, two hours, one hour, thirty minutes.

The detail: hand the task to someone else and the previous owner's pending reminders are deleted while the new owner's are written. Nobody is chased about work that stopped being theirs.

Recurring duties, six frequencies

Daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, half-yearly or yearly, with an owner, an end date or a fixed number of occurrences, and a completion window in hours or days.

The detail: creating the schedule also creates the first task immediately, so the person sees the work the same day rather than discovering the rota tomorrow.

Every task carries its own history

A message thread, an activity log and quality-control entries sit on each task, so the conversation about a job lives on the job rather than in someone's chat.

Deleting a task requires administrator rights, enforced by the database itself rather than by hiding the button — so it holds outside the app too.
04

WhatsApp

Ten operations · no app to open

Your staff already have it open

Ten operations run from chat: create a task, update one, complete one, delete one, list what is on your plate, look up a colleague, ask who is in today, apply for leave, approve or reject leave, and set up a recurring duty. The technician who will never install anything still gets counted.

Every one of the ten verifies the sender is an active member of that organisation before it does anything, and every task lookup is scoped to that organisation. A message cannot reach another company's data.

Names the way people actually type them

Ask for a colleague and partial names resolve — and the word order does not matter, so "panchal priyanka" and "priyanka panchal" both find the same person.

The detail: deactivated colleagues are filtered out of listings entirely, so a leaver stops being suggested as an assignee the day they are deactivated — rather than the day someone remembers to say so.

Chat does not mean unguarded

Completing or updating a task from WhatsApp is refused unless the task is yours — assigned to you or created by you.

The detail: "mark the report done" will not re-close an already-completed task by matching an old title, and approving leave from chat is refused to anyone who is not an administrator of that organisation. The rules are the same rules; only the window changed.

"Who's in today?"

One message returns the roll-call for a date: present, absent, late and early-out, with a headcount and an attendance percentage.

The detail: absentees are found by diffing the full roster against the punches — because an absent employee has no attendance row to read. A report built by listing the attendance table can never show you who is missing.
05

Pay

Largest module · statutory · payslip PDF

Salary structures that are dated

Each structure carries an effective-from and an effective-to with an annual CTC and its component lines, so a raise in the middle of the month resolves to the right structure rather than the latest one.

The detail: each component carries its own participates-in-PF-wage and participates-in-ESIC-wage flag, so an allowance you decided is liable stays liable — instead of the liability being guessed from the component's name.

Attendance drives pay directly

Working days, present days, paid leave, half days at half weight, loss of pay and payable days are resolved for the month, then carried onto the payroll run and stored alongside it.

The detail: the pro-rata factor is payable days ÷ working days, applied to earnings only, never to deductions. Prorating a deduction is how provident fund quietly comes up short and nobody notices until the annual return.

Statutory to the actual rules

Provident fund at twelve per cent from each side on Basic plus dearness allowance, capped at a fifteen-thousand wage. State insurance at 0.75 and 3.25 per cent while gross stays within twenty-one thousand. Professional tax on real slabs — Gujarat, Maharashtra, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh and Telangana, West Bengal, Rajasthan, Delhi at nil.

The detail: if the salary structure already contains a provident fund, insurance or professional tax line, the computed one is set to zero. The double deduction does not happen.

The payslip answers the question before it is asked

A real A4 PDF with your organisation's name and address, earnings and deductions separated, net pay, and the employer's own contributions shown below.

The detail: six attendance figures are printed on the slip itself — working days, present, paid leave, loss of pay, payable days, late count. That strip is what ends the "why is my salary short" conversation, because the answer is already in his hand. Each slip is filed to storage under the organisation and the month and the link is kept on the run, so reprinting last March costs one click, not one recalculation.

The biometric export, reviewed by a person

Upload the CSV or Excel the machine produces. Columns are matched to fields, rows are staged, and each is accepted or rejected by a human with a note.

The detail: a duplicate row, or one carrying any validation error, is not eligible for apply at all — you cannot bulk-accept your way past it. The batch moves uploaded → mapped → validated → applied as a state the database enforces, not a flag the screen sets. Three deliberate human acts stand between the file and payroll: accept the rows, confirm the batch, apply it. Nothing from that file reaches a payslip without all three.

Describe a salary structure and get a draft

Set out the structure in ordinary words and a draft comes back, split into components with annual amounts and deductions signed correctly.

Who approves it, stated plainly: the assistant writes into the form on screen and never into the database. Every component is re-checked against your own component codes, and anything it cannot match is shown to you as unmapped rather than quietly dropped. The record exists only after a person presses Save.
06

Appraisal

31 measured facts per person

The review is already written

For any period you choose — week, month, quarter or year — each employee resolves to thirty-one measured facts: tasks assigned and completed, average hours to completion, overdue count, days present against working days, late arrivals, early departures, average daily hours, leave requested and approved, and back-dated requests.

The detail: it spans four sources at once — the task board, the attendance table, the leave record and the conversation log. An appraisal built from task completion alone rewards the person who closes easy tickets and never turns up.

One score, and you can see its parts

Task completion at thirty per cent, attendance and punctuality at twenty-five each, leave planning and communication at ten each. Punctuality starts at a hundred and each late arrival or early departure costs half a day's worth against the days present.

The detail: the weighting normalises over the data that exists. Someone with no conversations recorded is not silently marked down for a category they were never measured in — a flaw that makes most composite scores unusable in their first quarter.

Print it and hand it over

A PDF report — employee, department, tasks completed, attendance rate, punctuality, overall score and key strengths — with a summary page carrying the averages across the set, and the top three performers named.

The same period exports as a twenty-two column spreadsheet, so the figure on the printed review can be traced back to the days that produced it rather than being taken on trust.

Strengths and gaps, stated as sentences

Each person's report labels what is working and what is not against fixed thresholds — attendance above ninety, punctuality above eighty, communication above seventy — and says so plainly rather than leaving a manager to read a number.

The detail: where there is not enough data to judge, it says "insufficient data" instead of printing a confident zero. The one thing that destroys a review meeting is a number nobody can defend.
The cost of the present arrangement

What you are losing today

  1. The second of the month. Two registers, one Excel sheet, and a supervisor who will "send the night-shift numbers by evening." Payroll waits for him. It waited for him last month too.
  2. The man who punched in at 9:50 at night and out at 6:10 in the morning reads as two half days. You correct it by hand. Every month. For every one of them.
  3. Salary day, and a message arrives: sir, ₹1,840 kam aaya. Nobody can say which day was cut or why. You pay the difference to end the conversation, and the register is never corrected.
  4. The leave that was "informed on WhatsApp" in April. The message is four thousand messages up a group chat. There is no record, so there is no argument to be had, so the day is paid.
  5. Appraisal week. You open a spreadsheet and try to remember eleven months, and end up writing the same three sentences for nine people — because the only person whose year you can actually recall is the one who was loudest.
Authority

Anyone can show you a dashboard.
Here is what it refuses to allow.

These are not warnings, validations or confirmation dialogs. They are refusals written into the database and the server, which means they hold when someone bypasses the screen — including when the instruction arrives from a chat window.

The attemptThe refusal
Running payroll on a month still open Blocked at the point of computation. Payroll period must be locked to finalize runs
Paying someone with no salary structure covering that month The run stops on that employee and names them, rather than paying a zero.
A second payroll run for the same person in the same period Refused by a uniqueness constraint. One run per person per period, structurally.
A salary structure of zero, or one whose end date precedes its start Both refused by check constraints on the compensation record.
Applying an imported attendance row that is a duplicate or carries an error Such rows are never eligible for the apply step. There is no override.
Completing or editing someone else's task from WhatsApp You can only complete tasks assigned to you or created by you
Approving leave without being an administrator Only admins can approve/reject leave requests — and an administrator sees only the requests routed to them, so approving your own is not possible to attempt.
Assigning work to someone in another organisation The assignee is verified independently of the sender. Assignee does not belong to this organization
Acting at all from a deactivated account Refused before anything else is evaluated. User is inactive
Punching in twice, or punching out with nothing open Both refused, with the open session named back to the employee.
Closing a night shift after two hours, or after twenty-two Refused outside the six-to-eighteen-hour window.
Punching outside the boundary, in strict mode The record is never written. The employee is told the distance in metres and the limit that applies.
Creating a task belonging to another organisation The organisation is stamped by the database, overriding whatever was sent. A task whose organisation cannot be resolved is rejected outright.
Deleting a task, or creating a user, without administrator rights Refused by row-level policy and on the server respectively — never by hiding a button.
On the roadmap

What is next, and what to run alongside for now.

Stated here because you would find it in the pilot anyway, and it is cheaper for both of us if you find it on this page first. Everything in the six pillars above is running today; the following is not yet.

Income tax deduction
Provident fund, state insurance and professional tax compute in full. TDS is not yet computed — it awaits annual projection logic. Run it alongside for now.
Statutory filing files
Per-employee PF, ESIC and PT figures compute and are stored on every run, so the numbers are ready. The ECR and challan file generators, and the period-level statutory totals screen, are still to come.
Leave balances
Requesting, routing, deciding, recording and notifying all work, and post-facto leave is flagged and scored. Entitlement, accrual and carry-forward balances are next; today, leave reaches payroll through the monthly override rather than deducting itself.
Overtime pay
Overtime hours are captured and printed on the slip. No rate is applied yet — overtime does not currently change net pay.
Employee payslip self-service
Administrators generate, download and reprint slips, and every slip is filed. The employee-facing screen and automatic distribution are still to come — slips are shared by the administrator today.
Unattended recurring generation
Recurring schedules are created with their first task, and the generator for later occurrences is written. Running it unattended on a timer is the remaining step.
Payroll change history
Recalculating a person replaces the previous run rather than versioning it. If a defensible run-by-run change trail matters to your auditors, weigh this one.
Working offline
The app requires connectivity to punch and to save. If any of your sites have poor coverage, test this before anything else — though staff at those sites can transact over WhatsApp instead.
The evaluation

Ninety minutes, with your last closed month.

  • 01Bring one real biometric export and one real salary structure — the messy ones, not the tidy ones.
  • 02Load the export. Accept the rows and reject the bad ones. Lock the period.
  • 03Open a payslip and read the six attendance figures printed on it: working days, present, paid leave, loss of pay, payable days, late count.
  • 04Set it beside the sheet you actually paid that month, and find where the two disagree. That difference is the whole argument, and it is either there or it is not.
  • 05Then send a WhatsApp message: assign a task to someone by half their name, ask who is in today, and apply for a day's leave. Watch it arrive in an administrator's queue with your name on it.
  • 06Finally, try to break it — run payroll on an unlocked month, complete a colleague's task from chat, approve your own leave, punch in twice. Confirm each refusal holds.
